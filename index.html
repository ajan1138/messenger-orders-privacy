Privacy Policy

Application: Messenger Orders
Last updated: May 17, 2025
Contact: ajanovicahmed11@hotmail.com

1. Introduction

Messenger Orders ("we", "our", or "the app") is an order management application that receives customer messages sent via Facebook Messenger, extracts structured order information using AI, and provides a dashboard to manage and fulfill those orders. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your personal information.

2. Data We Collect

When a customer sends a message to a Facebook Page connected to Messenger Orders, we may collect and process the following information:

  • Facebook user ID (PSID) and display name
  • Message text content
  • Order details extracted from messages (name, phone number, delivery address, product items)
  • Message timestamps

We do not collect passwords, payment card details, or any sensitive personal data beyond what is necessary to process an order.

3. How We Use Your Data

Data collected through Messenger Orders is used exclusively for the following purposes:

  • Processing and fulfilling customer orders received via Facebook Messenger
  • Sending automated order acknowledgement and confirmation messages to customers
  • Answering customer questions about products and delivery
  • Maintaining an order management dashboard for business operators

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond order fulfillment.

4. Third-Party Services

To operate the application, we share data with the following third-party services:

  • Meta (Facebook) — to receive and send messages via the Messenger platform. Subject to Meta's Privacy Policy.
  • Anthropic (Claude AI) — message text is sent to Anthropic's API to extract structured order information. Subject to Anthropic's Privacy Policy.

We do not sell your data to any third party.

5. Data Storage and Security

All data is stored in a secured PostgreSQL database. We apply industry-standard security practices including encrypted connections, access control, and JWT-based authentication for all dashboard access. Data is stored in the European Union or Bosnia and Herzegovina.

6. Data Retention

We retain order and message data for as long as it is necessary to fulfill orders and maintain business records. Customers may request deletion of their data at any time (see Section 7).

7. Your Rights and Data Deletion

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at ajanovicahmed11@hotmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

You may also use Facebook's built-in data deletion request feature. When you submit a deletion request through Facebook, we will delete all data associated with your Facebook user ID from our systems within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

Messenger Orders is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the application after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
